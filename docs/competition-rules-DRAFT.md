## 5. How to enter
1. Create a Highlightz account (free) and use Highlightz to catch clips from the Featured Streamers **during the competition dates**.
2. Post your clips publicly on **TikTok and/or Instagram** and keep those accounts public until prizes are paid.
3. Submit the links to your clips by the entries deadline using **[FORM / DISCORD CHANNEL]**, and tick the box agreeing to these Rules.
4. You may submit up to **5 clips**. Clips must be made from live streams inside the competition dates, not from old VODs, and must be yours to post.

A free account can watch one streamer at a time, so a free entrant may cover any one Featured Streamer.

## 6. How the winners are chosen
- **Clip score** = views + likes + comments. If the same clip is posted on both TikTok and Instagram, the two posts' numbers are added together. Shares are not counted.
- **Likes must be visible** on a post, or that post does not count.
- **Your total** = your **best 3** clips added together.
- Numbers are checked by Highlightz at the counting time in Section 2 and saved. Scores are not adjusted for differences between streamers or platforms.
- **Ties** are broken by the higher total views. If still tied, by the earlier submission.
- Before paying, we may ask top entrants for a **screen recording of their account analytics** to confirm the numbers.
